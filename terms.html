Yusuff Elektrikal is committed to transparent, honest pricing. All quotes are provided before work begins and will not change without your consent.

💵

Minimum RM50 call-out fee: Every site visit, regardless of job size or outcome, carries a minimum RM50 call-out fee. This covers Yusuff's travel time and on-site inspection. If you proceed with the repair on the same visit, this RM50 forms part of your total invoice — it is never charged in addition to the repair cost. If, after inspection, you choose not to proceed, the RM50 call-out fee remains payable for the time and travel already committed.

No surprises policy: If additional work is discovered mid-job that was not in the original quote, Yusuff will stop, explain the situation, and get your approval before proceeding. You will never be billed for work you didn't agree to.

  • The guarantee does not cover damage caused by misuse, power surges, flooding, tampering by a third party, or acts beyond our control after the job is completed.

  • Return visits made under this guarantee do not incur a new RM50 call-out fee — it was already paid on the original visit.
